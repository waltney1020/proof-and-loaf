Frequently asked questions

What is a good stocking stuffer for a bread baker?

Small tools they use every bake: a bread lame, a bench scraper, a dough whisk or an instant-read thermometer.

What is a good small gift for a beginner sourdough baker?

A straight-sided starter jar or a beginner-friendly bread book. Both help right away.

How do I gift sourdough starter?

Put a few spoonfuls of fed, active starter in a clean jar with a loose lid and a card with feeding instructions. Give it within a day or two, or tell them to refrigerate it.
