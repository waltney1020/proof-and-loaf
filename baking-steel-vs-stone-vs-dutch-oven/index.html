Baking Steel vs. Stone vs. Dutch Oven for Sourdough

Dutch ovens trap steam for you; steels and stones give you room and crisp bottoms. Here’s how to pick, and how to get great results from each.

The short answer

For a beginner baking one round loaf at a time, a Dutch oven or combo cooker is the easiest path to a great loaf, because it traps steam for you. A baking steel or stone shines if you also bake pizza, want to bake two loaves at once, or prefer long baguettes and batards, but you’ll need to add steam yourself.

How to add steam with a steel or stone

  • Cover the loaf with an upside-down roasting pan or large stainless bowl for the first 20 minutes, then remove it.
  • Or put a sturdy metal pan on the lowest rack while preheating and pour in hot water right after loading the bread. Protect your hands and the oven window from steam.
