Before you buy: one quick question

Ask (or quietly check) what they already bake with. Most sourdough bakers already own a scale and some kind of pot, so the best gifts tend to be the upgrades they keep putting off: a better lame, a proper banneton, a proofer for winter, or a book that takes them to the next level. If they’re brand new, a combo cooker or a good scale is the gift they’ll use every single bake.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a beginner sourdough baker?

The tools that make the first loaves easier: a digital scale, a straight-sided starter jar, a banneton, or a Lodge combo cooker. A beginner-friendly bread book is a safe pick too.

What do you get an experienced sourdough baker?

Upgrades they may not buy for themselves: a quality lame, a baking steel, a proofing box, or an in-depth book. Ask what they already own to avoid duplicates.

Can you give sourdough starter as a gift?

Yes. A spoonful of active starter in a clean jar, with feeding instructions, is a classic homemade gift. Pair it with a jar or a lame.

When should I buy sourdough gifts for the holidays?

Order by early December for the best shipping times, especially for heavy items like Dutch ovens.
