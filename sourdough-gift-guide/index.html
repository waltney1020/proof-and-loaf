Before you buy: one quick question

Ask (or quietly check) what they already bake with. Most sourdough bakers already own a scale and some kind of pot, so the best gifts tend to be the upgrades they keep putting off: a better lame, a proper banneton, a proofer for winter, or a book that takes them to the next level. If they’re brand new, a combo cooker or a good scale is the gift they’ll use every single bake.

Gift-giving tips

  • Order early. Heavy items like cast iron and baking steels can take longer to ship in December.
  • Check gift receipts. If you’re unsure whether they own one already, choose an item with easy returns.
  • Add a homemade touch. A jar of your own starter, or a printed favorite recipe, turns a tool into a thoughtful gift.
