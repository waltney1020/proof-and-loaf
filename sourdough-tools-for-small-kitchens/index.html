The small-kitchen rule: every tool does two jobs or folds away

Skip these in a small kitchen

  • A stand mixer. Sourdough uses gentle hand folds.
  • Multiple bannetons. One basket (or a bowl and towel) is plenty. See our banneton guide when you’re ready.
  • A bread box. A cut loaf keeps well cut-side down on a board for a day or two.

Small-kitchen workflow tips

  • Mix in the same container you bulk ferment in to save a bowl.
  • Keep your starter in a small jar and a small amount (say 50 g) to save fridge space.
  • Use parchment as a sling to move dough into the pot; no peel required.
