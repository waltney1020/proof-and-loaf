A plain cast iron pot bakes a loaf just as well as an heirloom one. These are the budget-friendly picks worth buying.

You don’t need an heirloom pot to bake great bread

The most expensive Dutch ovens are beautiful, but a plain cast iron pot bakes a loaf every bit as well. What matters for sourdough is a heavy, lidded vessel that tolerates high oven heat and is easy to load. Bare cast iron is usually the least expensive way to get there. How to make a budget pot work like a pricey one

  • Preheat fully. 30–45 minutes in a hot oven is typical.
  • Use a parchment sling to lower dough in safely.
  • Prevent a burnt bottom with a baking sheet on the rack below for the uncovered part of the bake.
  • Keep bare iron oiled lightly after washing so it doesn’t rust.
