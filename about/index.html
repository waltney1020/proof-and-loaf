About Proof & Loaf

Proof & Loaf is a small, independent site for people baking their first sourdough loaves at home. Our job is simple: help you buy the few tools that actually make a difference, and skip the ones that don’t.

What you’ll find here

Plain-English buyer guides that each answer one beginner question, such as what size Dutch oven to get or how to bake without one. Every guide includes a “you can skip this” option when one exists. See all guides.

How we research (and what we don’t claim)

Our picks are research-based. We compare manufacturer specifications, independent test-kitchen results (such as America’s Test Kitchen, Serious Eats and Wirecutter) and long-term owner feedback, then explain the tradeoffs for a beginner. We have not hands-on tested the products we list, and we never present research as personal testing. The full process is on our How we choose page.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t accept free products or payment in exchange for a recommendation or a better position on a list.
  • We don’t write fake reviews, invent test results or publish star ratings.
  • We don’t show prices, because they change daily. Use the buttons to see the current price on Amazon.

Who’s behind it

Proof & Loaf is run by a solo publisher with an AI-assisted research and writing workflow; every recommendation is checked against the sources named in the guide. Our social accounts use an AI-generated baker character, labeled as AI, who shares beginner tips and never claims to have tested gear. Spotted something wrong or out of date? Corrections make the guides better for everyone.

How the site makes money

Some links are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. It costs you nothing extra and never decides what we recommend. Details are on our affiliate disclosure page.
