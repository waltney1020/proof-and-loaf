About Proof & Loaf

Proof & Loaf is a small, independent site for people baking their first sourdough loaves at home. Our job is simple: help you buy the few tools that actually make a difference and skip the ones that don’t.

How we research

Every guide starts with the question a beginner actually has (“Do I really need a Dutch oven?”), then we work through manufacturer specifications, independent test-kitchen results, and the experience of long-time home bakers. We explain the tradeoffs in plain English and always include a budget option and a “you can skip this” option when one exists.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t accept free products or payment in exchange for a recommendation or a better position on a list.
  • We don’t write fake reviews or invent test results. Unless a guide says we tested something hands-on, our picks are research-based, and we cite who did the testing.
  • We don’t show prices, because they change daily. Use the buttons to see the current price on Amazon.

Who’s behind it

Proof & Loaf is run by a solo publisher with an AI-assisted research and writing workflow; every recommendation is checked against the sources named in the guide. Spotted something wrong or out of date? Corrections make the guides better for everyone.

How the site makes money

Some links are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. It costs you nothing extra and never decides what we recommend. Details are on our affiliate disclosure page.
